## Design Context

### Users
Kinet speaks to founders and product leaders who need serious digital products designed and engineered with care. They encounter the brand through the company site, portfolio work, and founder-shared social media, often while evaluating credibility and product judgment.

### Brand Personality
Precise, restrained, engineered. Kinet should feel quietly confident, established, and technically capable without startup hype, freelancer advertising, or self-congratulatory language.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light-first, editorial technology identity built from exact geometry, disciplined typography, generous negative space, and real product evidence. Preserve the existing Ink, Paper, Graphite, Mist, and Signal palette and existing logo construction. Signal is a sparse functional accent. Avoid generic SaaS templates, launch-announcement language, gold, gradients, neon, decorative 3D, glassmorphism, and fake claims.

### Design Principles
1. Let real work provide the proof; never substitute fabricated metrics, clients, awards, or testimonials.
2. Use hierarchy, alignment, cropping, scale, and whitespace before decoration.
3. Preserve Kinet's existing logo, type system, palette, geometry, and technical visual language.
4. Make every element earn its place and keep communication matter-of-fact.
5. Design for immediate credibility on a phone while retaining close-view precision.
